How it works

Four simple steps to math confidence

Every child follows the same clear path. You always know what comes next.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Assess

    A free 20-minute check finds the right starting level for your child.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Join a level

    Your child joins a small batch of up to 8 kids at the same level.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Practice & learn

    Two fun classes a week, plus 10 minutes of home practice a day.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Get certified

    Pass the level test, earn a certificate and move up to the next.
