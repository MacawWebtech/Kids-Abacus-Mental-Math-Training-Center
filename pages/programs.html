Programs for every level

Five clear levels take your child from first bead to mental math champion. Pick by skill, not just age.

The level roadmap

Each step up means bigger numbers, more speed and less need for the abacus.

  1. L1 Bead Starter Ages 5–6
  2. L2 Bead Explorer Ages 6–8
  3. L3 Bead Builder Ages 7–10
  4. L4 Bead Master Ages 8–12
  5. L5 Mind Champion Ages 9–14
