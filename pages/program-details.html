Level 3: Bead Builder

A 4-month course for ages 7–10 where kids master 3-digit sums and start solving them without the abacus.

Level 3 Ages 7–10 4 months 2 × 75 min a week

What your child will learn

Bead Builder is where the magic happens: kids stop counting on fingers and start seeing numbers in their mind.

  • Add and subtract any 3-digit numbers on the abacus, quickly and correctly.
  • Mental abacus — picturing the abacus in the mind to solve sums with no tool at all.
  • Listening sums — adding numbers the teacher reads aloud, which trains attention and memory.
  • Speed target: 20 three-digit sums a minute with 90%+ accuracy.

Curriculum timeline

  1. 1 Weeks 1–2

    Warm-up and level check

    Review 2-digit sums, set your child's starting speed and accuracy.

  2. 2 Weeks 3–5

    3-digit addition

    Move beads across three rods with small and big friends.

  3. 3 Weeks 6–8

    3-digit subtraction

    Take away with borrowing, still using bead friends.

  4. 4 Weeks 9–11

    Mental abacus

    Close eyes, picture the abacus, and solve sums in the mind.

  5. 5 Weeks 12–14

    Listening sums

    The teacher reads numbers aloud and kids add them mentally.

  6. 6 Weeks 15–16

    Practice tests & level test

    Two mock tests, then the Level 3 test and certificate.

A sample 75-minute class

Short blocks keep kids focused. Every class mixes new learning, practice and play.

10 min20 min20 min15 min10 min
  1. 10′

    Warm-up drill

    Quick finger exercises and 10 sums to get the brain going.

  2. 20′

    New idea

    The teacher shows one new bead move, step by step.

  3. 20′

    Abacus practice

    Kids practice the new move with worksheets, at their own pace.

  4. 15′

    Mental round

    Eyes closed: picture the beads and solve sums in the head.

  5. 10′

    Game & wrap-up

    A team speed game, stars for effort and the home task.
