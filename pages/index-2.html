How it works

One level, four simple steps

Here's exactly what the first 16 weeks look like.

  1. 1
    Step 1 · Week 1

    A friendly level check

    Your child does a relaxed 20-minute check with a teacher — no test stress. You get the result and a recommended level the same day.

    • Free, with the trial class
    • Checks speed, accuracy and focus
  2. 2
    Step 2 · Week 2

    Join a small batch

    Pick a batch time that suits your family. Your child joins up to 7 other kids at the same level, with one certified teacher.

    • Weekday, weekend or live online
    • Same teacher all level long
  3. 3
    Step 3 · Weeks 3–15

    Practice a little, often

    Two fun classes a week plus 10 minutes of home practice a day. You'll see what to practice in the parent app.

    • Weekly worksheets and games
    • Monthly report and teacher chat
  4. 4
    Step 4 · Week 16

    Earn the certificate

    Your child takes the level test in class. Pass, and they get a certificate at our monthly ceremony — then it's on to the next level.

    • Results in 3 working days
    • Families welcome at the ceremony
