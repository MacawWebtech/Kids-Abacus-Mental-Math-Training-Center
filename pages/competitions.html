How certification works

Simple, fair and the same for every child.

    Step 1: Practice

    Finish the level's lessons and home worksheets.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Take the test

    A 30-minute test of speed and accuracy, in class.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Get results

    Results in 3 working days, in your parent dashboard.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Celebrate

    Certificate at our monthly ceremony — families welcome!
