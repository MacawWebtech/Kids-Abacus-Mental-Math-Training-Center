Our story

About BeadUp Academy

We help kids fall in love with numbers using the abacus, patient teachers and a clear path.

Our mission

Every child can be good at math

Math fear is learned, so it can be unlearned. We replace it with speed, focus and the joy of getting it right.

The abacus turns numbers into something kids can touch and see. Once they can picture the beads in their mind, sums that once felt scary become a game.

Safe and kind

Background-checked staff and a calm, friendly space.

Clear progress

A level path and monthly reports. No guessing.

Small groups

Never more than 8 kids per teacher.

Parents as partners

Short home tasks and open teacher chats.

Beyond the beads

Skills your child takes everywhere

The abacus teaches math, but the habits it builds help in every class.

Sharper focus

Moving beads in a set order trains kids to stay on one task finish.

Faster thinking

Sums that took a minute on paper get solved in seconds, all in head.

Stronger memory

Holding the "mental abacus" in mind builds working memory day by day.

Better listening

Flash and oral sums teach kids to listen closely and respond with care.

Real confidence

Each level badge is proof they can do hard things, in math and beyond.

Better at school

Number sense carries over to fractions, word problems and test .

How we teach

Touch, picture, think

Three stages, used in every class at every level.

Touch

Real beads, real fingers. Kids feel how numbers are built.

Picture

Eyes closed, kids imagine the beads moving — the "mental abacus".

Think

Sums are solved in the mind, faster than with pen and paper.
