Our teaching method

Touch, picture, think

Kids learn best when they move from real objects to pictures to pure thinking. That's exactly how we teach.

1. Touch

Kids move real beads with the right fingers. Hands learn before the head.

2. Picture

They close their eyes and picture the same beads moving.

3. Think

Soon they solve sums in the mind, with no abacus at all.

4. Cheer

Every bit of effort gets noticed. Mistakes are part of learning.
